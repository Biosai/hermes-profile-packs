# Exit checklist

Each step is the owner's, written with its exact inputs and a check that proves it done.

1. **Export the full history** (trades, deposits, withdrawals, conversions, staking, fees) for every year the account was open, plus a statement at the exit date. It disappears when the account closes, and the books and the accountant need it. Done when the files are in the books' documents folder.
2. **Choose the receiving wallet**: one whose register row has the same owner as the money. A company's money goes to a wallet that holds only that company's money.
3. **Pick the network per token.** Majors on their native chain or a cheap layer 2 the exchange supports for withdrawal. Long-tail tokens on one cheap common network, then consolidated with a decentralised aggregator when conversion on the exchange is blocked.
4. **Check the withdrawal fee and minimum** of each line. A line worth less than its fee or below the minimum is written off as dust and listed as a loss, not chased.
5. **Order**: tokens tagged for delisting or monitoring first, then the largest lines.
6. **Test transfer** of a small amount on each new network and address before the full amount. Done when it arrives and matches.
7. **Full transfer**, then check the receiving wallet on chain.
8. **Book** each transfer in the owner's ledger, linked to the exchange's history line.
9. **Close or keep** the account per the owner's decision, after the last statement is saved.

## Common traps
- The exchange's in-app "convert" may be disabled for some regions: confirm before relying on it.
- Some networks need a memo or tag; a missing memo can lose the funds.
- A token's ticker may exist on several networks with different contracts: use the contract the exchange lists for that network.
