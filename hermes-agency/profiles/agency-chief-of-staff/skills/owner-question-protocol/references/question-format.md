# The question format

One decision, one message, the question first.

```
Decision needed: <one line>
Context: <two lines at most>
A) <option>: <consequence, cost, reversible?>
B) <option>: <consequence, cost, reversible?>
Recommendation: <letter>, because <reason, with the number and its source, or "no data: assumption">.
Blocks: <link>. Meanwhile: <what continues>. Default on no answer: <safe default>.
```

- Two to four options. Each says what happens, what it costs (money, time, risk, what gets dropped) and whether it can be undone.
- The safe default is never irreversible.
- A question about a draft carries the draft's full text below the block.
- Text the owner must copy is ready to paste, apart from any commentary.

## Several open questions
Order them by how much work each one blocks. Ask the first. Ask the next only when the previous one is answered or deferred: an answer often changes or removes the questions behind it. Keep the others in your notes with what each blocks, not in the message.

## After the answer
1. Record the answer verbatim where the question lives.
2. Record the decision, with its date, where decisions live: the project brief, `decision-log` or the work item.
3. Act, and say what you did.
