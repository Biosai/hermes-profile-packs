# The evidence plan

Below about one signup a week, a copy change cannot be measured for months. Gather evidence instead of rewriting or A/B testing.

## When the loss comes after the first use: a free self-test first
Needs the owner's standing approval for probes on internal test accounts.
1. Sign up as a stranger would.
2. Bring the three situations real people arrive with, in the main languages of the audience.
3. Grade the first experience of each: did it solve the situation, how long was the answer or the path, what did the product ask for next.
4. Hand over the transcripts or recordings and a verdict.

A weak first experience is a content fix, not a feature.

## Then about five real people
Each tries the product on a situation of their own, then answers three questions:
1. Is it better than the free substitute you use today?
2. Would you come back tomorrow, and for what?
3. What would make you pay the price?

Rewrite the proposition in their words, then carry those words to the site, the store listing and the directory entries.

## Reading the result
- Quote, do not paraphrase into the team's vocabulary.
- Give counts and the window: "four of five used it once", never a percentage of five.
- "Used exactly once" points at the first experience. "Used for a week, then left" points at the return loop. They need different fixes.
- No reply to a feedback request means there is no written evidence of why people left. It does not mean nothing is wrong.

## The edge
- Test the proposition against "why this instead of the free substitute?", the substitute often being a general-purpose tool the customer already has. A list of use cases does not answer it.
- An edge the substitute can copy quickly is not one. State an edge as a hypothesis built on what the substitute structurally lacks, and sequence it after the return loop: it pays only once someone comes back.

## When the conversation turns to another decision
If the owner moves on to a different decision, such as stopping a channel, answer that decision. Do not push the evidence plan again.
