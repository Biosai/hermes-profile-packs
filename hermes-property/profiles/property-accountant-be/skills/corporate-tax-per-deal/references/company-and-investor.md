# The company, the investor personally, and annual accounts

## Corporate income tax, when the investor acts through a company

Property held as inventory or as a fixed asset, depreciation, capital gain,
reduced rate under conditions. How the investor wants tax shown (per deal or
at year end) is one of the investor's rules: read it in the investor file.

## The investor personally

Rent the investor receives from their company, risk of requalification,
letting in their own name: flag them; there is often a
company-versus-individual trade-off.

## Reading annual accounts

The Centrale des bilans of the National Bank of Belgium publishes annual
accounts: read them for a target company, a seller, a commercial tenant.

## Rules

- **The accountant's written answer turns an option into a rule**, and only
  for the case it covers. An answer about short-term letting does not apply to
  long-term letting. Record it in the same conversation, with its source, in
  the investor file (setting `property.investor_file`) or through
  `property-coordinator`.
- **The investor's own spreadsheets are checked too.** A spreadsheet that
  mixes depreciation alone with a property's overall result compares two
  different things: say so.
- **Before tax is said out loud.** A cash flow or a profit without tax carries
  the label "before tax".
