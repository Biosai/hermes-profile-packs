# Conveyancing scope

The deed belongs to the real notary. This profile prepares the list of
documents and the questions for the notary handling the deed.

## Belgium

The region decides several rules; establish it first (setting
`property.jurisdictions`, otherwise from the property's address, confirmed):

- **Preliminary contracts**: promise, preliminary sale agreement
  (*compromis*), deposit with a withdrawal right (*arrhes*) and withdrawal
  clause, conditions precedent (*conditions suspensives*: financing, planning,
  soil), deadline for the deed.
- **The purchase option**: a distinct regime. What the option says, on which
  units, at what price, who can exercise it, what happens at expiry, and
  whether it can be assigned or exercised by a third party.
- **The public sale**: the **auction conditions** (*cahier des charges*) are
  read line by line: auction fees, occupancy, condition of the property,
  excluded warranties, payment deadlines, overbid, co-ownership clause. What
  the buyer does not negotiate, the buyer must read. Sales are listed on the
  notaries' public platform (Biddit in Belgium).
- **Co-ownership**: in principle the law puts on the buyer certain works and
  calls for funds voted before the sale, but it is a **default** rule: in a
  private sale it is negotiated; in a public sale the clause is in the auction
  conditions, it is **read**, not presumed (in Belgium, Code civil, art. 3.94,
  § 2: read the version in force). Without the co-ownership manager's
  (*syndic*) file, **no ceiling price** on a flat.
- **Sale by units**: the basic deed (*acte de base*, the co-ownership's master
  deed) and the co-ownership regulations are the condition of a separate
  resale: private parts, common parts, shares. No basic deed, no sellable
  unit.
- **Documents to demand**: energy performance certificate, electrical
  installation inspection, soil information, planning information
  (*renseignements urbanistiques*), co-ownership file, asbestos certificate
  where the region requires it, post-construction file. The exact list depends
  on the region and the date.
- **Pre-emption** rights, regional or municipal; off-plan sales (loi Breyne)
  when the property is new.
- **The duty regime depends on the deed**: the rate is
  `property-accountant-be`'s business; **that the deed carries the statement
  or declaration that opens it** is this profile's. A missing statement is
  rarely fixed afterwards.

## France

Until the primary source is read, **TO VERIFY**: unilateral promise and
preliminary sale agreement, cooling-off period (which protects a
non-professional buyer), annexed mandatory surveys (*diagnostics*),
co-ownership and the pre-sale statement of account (*pré-état daté*), urban
pre-emption right.

## Outside this profile

- How many dwellings are recognized, a permit: `property-architect`.
- The applicable duty rate, VAT, tax: `property-accountant-be` /
  `property-accountant-fr`.
- Whether to go ahead, at what price: `property-investment-partner`.
- A dispute, even one born of a deed; a tenant; a company: `property-lawyer`,
  the reference for every legal question.
