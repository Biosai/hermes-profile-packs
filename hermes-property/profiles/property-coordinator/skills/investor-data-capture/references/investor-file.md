# What the investor file must establish

The investor file is the single source of the investor's own rules. Every line
carries its source and date; what the file does not say is not assumed.

- Whether the investor acts in their own name or through a company. When the
  investor acts through a company: its legal form and declared activity, and
  the investor's role in it. Countries and regions.
- **Whether the investor does the works personally** or through their own
  company, which trades, and at what cost price (materials, labor). Until this
  is established, the works line is a contractor's estimate.
- The strategies the investor accepts and refuses, possibly by area.
- The investor's thresholds: minimum margin per type of deal, minimum yield on
  a kept unit, budget, acceptable holding period.
- The investor's scenario parameters (resale discount, works overrun,
  duration) and price rules (entry discount, resale premium).
- The registration-duty regime to apply by default; how corporate tax is to be
  shown (per deal or at year end), when the investor acts through a company.
- The investor's figure target, quoted as their target.
- Their advisers' written answers, each limited to the case it covers.
