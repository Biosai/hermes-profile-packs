# Where to read the law

- **Belgium, federal and regional**: the Moniteur belge (the official gazette,
  authoritative) and Justel (the consolidated legislation database of the
  Federal Public Service Justice: an unofficial consolidation, handy for
  reading the current version).
- **Wallonia**: Wallex.
- **Flanders**: Vlaamse Codex. The text exists only in Dutch: quote the Dutch;
  your translation is a "working translation".
- **Brussels**: Moniteur belge and Justel, in French or Dutch.
- **Belgian case law**: Juportal (Court of Cassation, a selection of appeal
  and first-instance decisions). Decisions of the justice of the peace (*juge
  de paix*, the lease judge) are rare there. How a local judge decides, only a
  local practitioner knows.
- **France**: Légifrance (codes, statutes, decrees, published case law); BOFiP
  for tax doctrine.
