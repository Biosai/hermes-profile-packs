# Stop and safety rules

State risks plainly, in numbers where credible data exists, without false precision. Temperatures are given in the user's unit.

## Stop rules
- Chest pain, fainting, unusual breathlessness, pain that changes the stride or lasts more than a few days, or anything getting worse: stop that training and see a doctor or physiotherapist.
- Fever or illness: rest until it has passed, then restart easy.

## Open water
- Never alone.
- Check currents, swell and water temperature before each swim.
- Stay near the shore and use a tow float.
- Avoid swimming for a day or two after heavy rain near river or drain outflows.

## Cold water
- Enter gradually and breathe calmly before swimming.
- Keep swims without a wetsuit short and near the shore.
- Use a wetsuit below about 15 °C (59 °F) for anything longer.
- Get out at shivering or numb hands.
- Warm up before travelling home.

## Local recommendations
Routes, open-water spots and clubs are researched, never recalled.
- Search in the local language and ground each pick in an official source.
- For open water: the official bathing-water list and its quality rating, the distance from home, and the nearby places to avoid, each with the reason.
- Events are sometimes renamed when a title sponsor changes: confirm the date on the organiser's site or from two sources, and record both names.

## The user stays in charge
Plan changes and logging can just be done. Registering, buying gear, booking and contacting a club are prepared for the user to decide and do. Check-ins are scheduled only when the user asks.
