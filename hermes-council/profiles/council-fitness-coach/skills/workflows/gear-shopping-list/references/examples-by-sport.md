# Examples by sport

What to ask, what comes first, and where conditions decide. These are examples of the method, not a catalogue: for any other sport, ask the same four questions (what keeps the user safe, what must fit, what do conditions change, what does the user already own). Sizes differ between brands: use the brand's own chart with the user's measurements, in the user's units.

## Open-water swimming
- **Safety first**: a bright tow float and a bright cap. Choose a float with a dry compartment when keys or a phone come along; the phone goes in a waterproof pouch inside it, tested once with paper before it is trusted.
- **Sizing to ask**: height, weight and chest for a wetsuit.
- **Where conditions decide**: a thin wetsuit is fine in autumn water and cold in winter. Give a budget option for the mild months and a whole-season option, and name your pick.
- **As the water cools**: a wetsuit, then a neoprene cap and socks, and a changing robe to warm up afterwards.
- **Fit-critical**: the wetsuit. Recommend trying it on in a shop.

## Cycling
- **Safety first**: a helmet, and lights when the user rides at dusk or in the dark.
- **Sizing to ask**: head circumference for a helmet; for an inner tube, the size printed on the tyre's sidewall and the valve type.
- **Where conditions decide**: tyres and clothing for wet or cold months against fair-weather riding only.
- **Check from a shop photo**: an inner tube must cover the tyre's width and have the matching valve. Read both on the box.

## Running
- **Safety first**: something that makes the runner visible when running in the dark.
- **Sizing to ask**: the shoe size in the sizing system the user knows, and the surface (road or trail).
- **Where conditions decide**: one pair for dry roads against a pair that also copes with wet or muddy ground.
- **Fit-critical**: the shoes. Recommend trying them on in a shop.

## Board sports
- **Safety first**: for skating, a helmet and pads; for surfing, a leash in good condition.
- **Sizing to ask**: height, weight and level, which decide the size of the board.
- **Where conditions decide**: a board or a wetsuit for one season against one that covers the year.
