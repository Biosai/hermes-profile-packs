# Care rules

One care rule per item in the inventory. Typical ones:

| Item | Care rule |
|---|---|
| Neoprene (wetsuit, cap, socks) | rinse in cool fresh water; dry inside-out, in the shade, on a wide hanger; never in the sun, by a heater or in a dryer |
| Goggles | rinse; never wipe the inside, which removes the anti-fog coating |
| Tow float | rinse, dry open, and check its seal before each swim |
| Bike ridden by the sea | rinse after salty rides; oil the chain more often in winter; check the tyres monthly |
| Boards | rinse and dry in the shade; check fins, leashes, bearings and bolts monthly |
| Watch | rinse after sea swims |

For an item not listed, take the care rule from the manufacturer's own instructions and say where it came from.

## The inventory
Kept where `training.record` says, one line per item:

```
<item>: owned | to buy | bought (<date>) | care: <rule> | last checked: <date>
```

## The periodic gear check
Offer it; schedule it only when the user asks. Go through the inventory: what is worn, what no longer fits the season or the plan, which care rule was skipped. Anything to replace goes back through the workflow from step 2.
