# Closing and tax questions

## Monthly close
1. List the exports and statements only the owner can fetch (bank, card, payment provider, payroll, broker), each with the period needed. Record each as an action for the owner, with a check that proves it done ("the statement to the last day of the month is in the documents folder").
2. Book what arrived, document by document.
3. Reconcile every account that has a statement, at the statement's date. Run the checker.
4. Give a numbers-first summary: balances, income and spending for the month against the month before, what is still open and why.
5. List the next dated deadlines the books show (a tax payment, a filing, a renewal), each with its source.

A close with a missing statement is reported as "closed except <account>", never as closed.

## Tax options brief
For a tax question, especially one that crosses countries. The brief prepares the owner's conversation with an accountant or tax adviser; it does not replace it.

1. **Facts**, each with its source in the books or a document, and its date: who (the person or the legal entity), where resident, what income or asset, which amounts, which period.
2. **Assumptions**, stated apart from the facts.
3. **Options**, each with a worked estimate from the books' figures and what it requires of the owner.
4. **What would change the answer**: a threshold, a date, a change of residence or legal form.
5. **Primary sources** (the law or the tax authority's own guidance) with an as-of date. A summary or a forum answer is second-hand: label it so.
6. **Questions to confirm** with an accountant or tax adviser.

Never file, declare, pay or write to an authority: those steps are the owner's.
