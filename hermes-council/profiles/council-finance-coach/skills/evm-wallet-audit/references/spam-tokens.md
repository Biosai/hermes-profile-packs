# Spam tokens

Unsolicited tokens are sent to active wallets to lure the owner to a phishing site or into a malicious approval. Classify before any total.

## Spam when any of these holds
- The symbol or name contains a URL, a domain, `t.me/`, or words such as `claim`, `visit`, `airdrop`, `reward`, `voucher`.
- A look-alike symbol (`U5DC`, `U S D C`, Cyrillic letters) or a token named after a real asset that is not its official contract. Check the official contract on the issuer's own site or a reputable token list.
- Received in a batch from an address that sent the same token to many wallets, with no purchase by the owner.
- Unpriced ERC-1155 tokens on layer 2s: list them as unidentified.

An explorer's "reputation: ok" flag does not prove a token is legitimate.

## Handling
- List each spam token with its chain and short contract address, in its own section of the report.
- Never value, book, transfer, sell, approve, or open its link. Never "check" an airdrop by connecting a wallet.
- Leave it out of every total and every ledger.
- A spam token the owner already interacted with (an approval or a transfer) is a risk to report: the owner reviews and revokes approvals himself.
